📖 How to Play Beastmaster's Arena

A strategic auto-battler — build the perfect team and survive endless waves!

🎯 The Goal

Defeat as many waves of enemies as possible. Each wave gets harder, but you earn more gold to buy stronger beasts. Your Highest Wave Reached is your score!

🛒 Step 1: Recruit Beasts

  • The Beast Bazaar shows 5 random beasts for sale
  • Click any beast to buy it (if you have enough 🪙 gold)
  • Spend 5 🪙 to reroll the shop for new beasts
  • You can own up to 12 beasts in your Menagerie

🗑️ Step 2: Discard (NEW!)

  • Hover any beast in your Menagerie to see a red ✖ button
  • Click it to sell the beast back to the bazaar
  • You get half the original cost back in gold 🪙
  • Useful when your roster is full or you found a better beast!

🎯 Step 3: Position Your Army

  • Drag beasts from your Menagerie onto the 6 arena slots
  • You have 3 Front Row slots (Tanks — take hits)
  • You have 3 Back Row slots (Ranged — deal damage safely)
  • Each beast has a preferred role (Front/Back) shown on shop cards
  • Double-click a deployed beast to recall it back to the Menagerie

✨ Step 4: Build Synergies

When multiple beasts share a Type or Class, they unlock powerful bonuses:

  • Goblin (2/4) → +20% / +50% Attack
  • Beast (2/4) → +15% / +40% HP
  • Undead (2/4) → 25% / 50% Lifesteal (heal on hit)
  • Demon (2/4) → +30% / +70% Crit Chance
  • Dragon (2) → +25% ALL stats 🐉
  • Human (2) → +20% HP & Attack
  • Warrior (2/4) → +30% / +60% Armor (reduces damage)
  • Mage (2/4) → +30% / +70% Spell Power (Mage damage)
  • Ranger (2) → +40% Crit Damage

Look at the Synergy Bar above the arena to see what's active!

⚔️ Step 5: Combat

  • Click ⚔️ BEGIN BATTLE to start the auto-battle
  • Beasts attack automatically based on their ATK stat
  • Front-row beasts prioritize attacking enemy front row
  • Ranged beasts can pick any target
  • Critical hits show in gold, healing in green
  • If ALL your beasts die, the game ends
  • If ALL enemies die, you win the wave + gold!

💎 Beast Rarities

Common 3🪙 — Basic stats, easy to find
Rare 6🪙 — Solid mid-game beasts
Epic 12🪙 — Strong synergy enablers
Legendary 24🪙 — Game-winning monsters 👑

Higher waves = better odds of rare/epic/legendary beasts appearing in the shop!

💡 Pro Tips

  • Focus a synergy: 4 Goblins = +50% ATK is HUGE
  • Mix front + back: Pure ranged dies to enemy frontline fast
  • Save gold: Sometimes it's worth rerolling for a Legendary
  • Discard weaklings: Free up roster space for stronger beasts
  • Lifesteal Undead are amazing tanks in the back row
  • Dragon Knight + Ancient Dragon = +25% to everything! 🔥
