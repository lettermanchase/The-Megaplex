ℹ️ HOW WAGERS WORK

Read this before you challenge!
⚔️ The Basics
  • Both players bet the same amount of tokens.
  • You and your opponent each play the chosen game.
  • Highest score (or lowest, for time-based games) wins the pot.
  • You have 24 hours after the wager is accepted to play and submit.
🔴 Connect 4 Battles
  • Connect 4 is a live head-to-head game — both players play on the same board in real time.
  • The first to connect 4 discs wins the pot (minus 10% house cut).
  • If the board fills with no winner, it is a draw and both are refunded (minus 5% each).
  • Games expire after 1 hour if incomplete — the player whose turn it was forfeits.
🏆 Payouts
  • Winner: Takes the full pot minus a 10% house cut.
  • Tie / Draw: Both players refunded minus 5% each.
  • No-show / expired: Player who did not act forfeits.
  • You must click "CLAIM" on the friends page to collect your winnings.
⛔ Other Rules
  • You can only challenge friends — send a friend request first.
  • Tokens are escrowed the moment you challenge or accept.
  • Cancelling a pending challenge refunds you immediately.
